Executive Summary
Current State

Membership: The member base grew to 195 across the quarter from 189 in July, with the active rate at 85.64% in September after reaching 87.96% in August. Suspended members fell from 36 to 28 over the period. Attrition held below the sub-7% target in every month, finishing at 3.13%.

Revenue: Gross revenue totalled $154,270 for the quarter, easing from $52,785 in July to $50,321 in September. Membership revenue of $124,733 made up 80.9% of the total and held between $41,166 and $41,804 each month. Intro offer revenue fell to $1,786 in September from $3,233 in August.

Utilisation: Booking utilisation stayed below the 75-85% band, ending the quarter at 58.34% after a high of 62.15% in August. Attendance utilisation closed at 52.73% against the 70-75% range. The booking-attendance gap stayed at 6.62% or under in all three months, inside the 10% maximum.

Sales vs Cancellations: Sales exceeded cancellations in each month, with 39 memberships sold against 29 cancelled across the quarter. September recorded 11 memberships against 6 cancellations, while intro offers fell from 110 to 39 and first visits from 121 to 70 after the discounted offer period ended.

Key Issues
  • Membership conversion: Memberships sold fell from 16 in August to 11 in September, while intro offers fell from 110 to 39.
  • Booking utilisation below target: At 58.34%, booking utilisation sits below the 75-85% band, and sessions offered declined from 486 to 456.
  • Attendance utilisation below range: At 52.73% in September, attendance utilisation sits below the 70-75% healthy range.
  • Suspended members: Suspended members rose from 23 in August to 28 in September, lowering the active rate from 87.96% to 85.64%.
  • Lead volume: First visits fell from 121 to 70 in September, with no discounted intro offer running 10 August to 27 September.
  • Package revenue: Packages declined each month, from $6,595 in July to $5,403 in September, while pack units sold rose to 79.
Active Interventions Being Deployed
  • 50% Off The STRONG Experience: Live 28 September to 11 October for new and lapsed prospects, using Hapana packages and Grow emails to rebuild intro offer and first visit volume.
  • FOH one-on-one sales training: Rolling out since late August to build FOH sales capability and lift membership conversion from intro offer and first visit volume.
  • FOH rostering consistency: Holding FOH shifts to the existing guidelines so every intro offer lead receives consistent follow-up, addressing the gaps that limited membership conversion.
  • EOSR report and trainer sales capture: Deploying into September to give daily visibility of sales activity and capture membership opportunities trainers identify in sessions.
  • Movement Block I: Running 21 September to 25 October with updated class schedules and erg custom lists, intended to support booking volume toward the 75-85% band.
Expected Outcomes
  • Membership conversion: Lift membership sales relative to intro offer volume through consistent FOH follow-up and one-on-one sales training, as 50% Off The STRONG Experience adds lead volume.
  • Net membership growth: Support membership sales continuing to exceed cancellations, building total membership from 195 through the quarter ahead.
  • Attrition: Hold attrition below the sub-7% target from 3.13% in September while new member volume increases.
  • Booking utilisation: Rebuild booking utilisation from 58.34% toward the 75-85% band through the STRONG-ER Series and Black Friday sale.
  • Suspended volume: Reduce suspended members from 28 toward the 23 recorded in August to support the active rate.

06
Summary & Path Forward

Castle Hill closed the quarter with 195 members, attrition under the sub-7% target in all three months and gross revenue of $154,270, with sales ahead of cancellations each month. Utilisation remained below both benchmarks, at 58.34% booking and 52.73% attendance in September, and suspended members stood at 28.

The binding constraint is membership conversion from a smaller base of intro offers, alongside booking volume. 50% Off The STRONG Experience is live to 11 October for new and lapsed prospects, supported by HQ emails through Grow, and should rebuild lead volume for FOH follow-up. The STRONG-ER Series (26 October to 22 November), the Black Friday sale (9 to 30 November) and Movement Block K (from 30 November) give the studio further reasons to lift bookings. Sales ahead of cancellations should, over time, support total membership growth and booking utilisation.

Key Takeaways
  • Membership reached 195 with attrition below sub-7% in every month.
  • Booking and attendance utilisation remain below target at 58.34% and 52.73%.
  • Membership conversion is the binding constraint on a smaller intro offer base.
  • 50% Off The STRONG Experience, live to 11 October, supports lead volume.
